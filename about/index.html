The Science

IQ Test (Fluid Intelligence)

40 questions across number series (sequential reasoning), visual matrix patterns (spatial intelligence) and numerical analogies. Scored on the standard scale with a mean of 100 and a standard deviation of 15.

Reaction Time

Based on simple visual reaction time research. The average adult response falls between 250 and 270 milliseconds; the distribution data comes from large public datasets.

Working Memory (Digit Span)

Miller's 1956 research found that people hold about seven items, plus or minus two, in working memory. The digit span task is the classic way of measuring that limit.

Big Five Personality

Questions adapted from the IPIP Big-Five Factor Markers (Goldberg, 1992). The five-factor model is the most replicated structure in personality psychology.

Stroop Effect (Focus)

First described by J. R. Stroop in 1935. The interference effect measures executive function and cognitive control, and it is one of the most reproducible findings in the field.

Our Approach to Cognitive Testing

CoreSkillAI builds free, anonymous versions of classic instruments from experimental psychology — digit span, matrix reasoning, the Stroop task and the five-factor model — and presents them in a form that takes minutes rather than an afternoon.

No account is required, results are computed on your device, and nothing you enter is sold or shared. Percentiles are calculated against anonymised aggregate score distributions. These tools are for education and self-exploration; they are not clinical instruments, and no online test can replace professional psychological or medical assessment.
