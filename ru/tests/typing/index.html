What Typing Speed Measures

Typing speed in words per minute (one "word" = five characters) reflects motor learning, muscle memory and reading-ahead skill. Average computer users type 35-45 WPM; professional typists reach 65-80; competitive typists exceed 120. Accuracy matters as much as raw speed — every correction costs more time than typing slowly and cleanly.

How to improve

Touch typing with all ten fingers and eyes on the screen is the single biggest upgrade, typically doubling speed within months of deliberate practice. Consistent daily practice of 15-20 minutes beats occasional marathons; focus on accuracy first and let speed follow.

Why does my WPM vary between tests? Text difficulty, punctuation density, keyboard quality and fatigue all matter. Averaging several runs gives a truer picture.

Is faster always better? Beyond ~60 WPM, typing rarely bottlenecks knowledge work — but the comfort of effortless typing frees attention for thinking.
