Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026. This policy explains what coreskillai.com records, what the third-party services on the site do, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible

CoreSkillAI is run by Eduard-Dragos Condria, an individual based in Romania (European Union). There is no company behind the site. For any privacy question write to ocultmaster9@gmail.com.

What the tests record

What we collect: When you finish the IQ, Pattern or Mental Rotation test we record how it went: which puzzle rules appeared, whether each was answered correctly, how long each took, your total score, your language and the date. We do not record your name, email, IP address, or any account or device identifier, and we set nothing that would let us recognise you if you came back.

The other tests (reaction time, working memory, colour vision, Big Five, emotional intelligence, focus and typing) are scored entirely in your browser. Their answers and results are not sent to us.

No account exists on this site, so we hold no profile, password or email list.

Why this data is collected

Why: This test has no standardisation sample yet. Response data is the only way to measure how hard each puzzle actually is and how well it separates strong reasoning from weak, which is what turns a plausible-looking score into a meaningful one. It is not used for anything else and is never sold.

Cookies and local storage

We keep your theme and language choice in your browser's local storage. We write nothing else to your device. The cookies you may see on this site are set by the two Google services described below, not by us.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this site or to other websites. Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners show ads based on those visits.

  • You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising from other participating vendors at aboutads.info or, in Europe, at youronlinechoices.eu.
  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. See also Google's privacy policy.

Advertising does not influence the tests, the scoring or the articles.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see which pages get visited. Google collects that on its own terms, which include approximate location and device type. We see totals only, not individual visitors. You can block it with Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Your choices and rights

Your data: Because the test records contain nothing that identifies you, we cannot locate, export or delete an individual person's entry — there is no link back to you. If you would rather nothing were collected, simply do not finish the test: nothing is sent until your result appears.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the tests keep working without them. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, data-protection law gives you rights of access, correction, deletion and objection over personal data. For data held by Google, use the Google links above. For anything you have emailed to us, write to the address below and it will be deleted on request. You may also complain to your national data-protection authority; in Romania that is the ANSPDCP.

Children

The site is written for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact form and email

Contact form: The form on the contact page opens your own email app. It does not send anything to this site. Messages you email us are used only to reply to you.

Changes to this policy

If what the site records changes, this page is updated first and the date at the top changes with it.
